Quick Guide (Casters & Crew) Producer Manual

Quick Guide

Welcome to the Stream Production Manager. If you are a caster, stage crew member, or general production staff, this one-page guide will give you a quick overview of how the system works and what to look out for.

The Big Picture

The Producer runs the show using a detailed timeline. As they move from one segment to the next, your screen will automatically update in real-time. You never need to refresh the page!

1. The Caster View

The Caster View is your digital run-of-show. It has two main columns:

  • ON AIR NOW (Left): This is what is currently happening live on the stream. It has a ● LIVE indicator and a bright green glowing border.
  • UP NEXT (Right): This is a preview of the next segment. It helps you prepare for the upcoming transition.

2. Task Lists

Sometimes, a segment isn't a single event, but a checklist of items that must be completed before moving on. These are called Task Lists.

If you see tasks appearing with checkboxes, it means the Producer is working through a checklist (like running ads, rolling credits, or checking audio levels). You will see the checkboxes fill in automatically as the Producer marks them complete.

3. Producer Messages

The Producer has the ability to send direct, urgent messages to the Caster/Crew screens. When the Producer sends a message, a vibrant purple banner will slide down from the top of your screen.

Visual Example: Producer Message
📣
Message from Producer
Stretch! We are delaying the next match by 2 minutes for a bathroom break.

You do not need to dismiss this message. The Producer will clear it from their end when the information is no longer needed.

4. Stream Status Indicators

Keep an eye on the overall stream status, which may be displayed on your monitor:

  • IDLE - The production has not started yet.
  • LIVE - The stream is actively running and you should act accordingly.
  • PAUSED - The production is paused (usually for an extended break or technical issue).
